Open any local port

Opens 127.0.0.1:<port> as a proxied tab in the portal (sidebar group Leases). The lease lives while this page is open: it dies 30 s after the connection drops, and never lasts past 8 h. Reloads and tab switches within the grace period are safe. Some ports (SSH, databases, Caddy admin, …) are denied — see the portal ports-deny.yaml.
